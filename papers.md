## Consider writing your next scientific paper in MyST instead of Microsoft Word,

MyST (<https://mystmd.org>) is an open-source markup language designed for scientific documents. [Curvenote](http://curvenote.com) is Word-style WYSIWYG editor built on top of open-source MyST. Articles written in MyST or Curvenote can be exported to any format including $\LaTeX$, JATS and Word, and are thereby more future-compatible.
